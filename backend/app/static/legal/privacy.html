Last updated {updated}. This explains what Squared ({operator}, "we") collects, why, and your choices. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google or Apple, your name, your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead) and an account identifier from them; optionally your UPI ID. We never see your Google or Apple password, and we don't collect your phone number.
  • What you add: groups, friends you split with, expenses, splits, payments you record, comments, chat messages, budgets and bill photos.
  • Device: a push notification token, app version, and an anonymous device identifier used to prevent coin abuse.
  • Usage: events such as "expense confirmed" or "invite shared", used to understand and improve the app. We don't use advertising trackers.

Bill scanning reads the photo on your phone; only the photo you choose to attach is uploaded.

Why we use it

  • To run the service: show balances and notify the people involved.
  • To run the coins programme fairly, including detecting fake accounts and collusion.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We don't sell your data and don't share it for advertising.

Who sees it

  • People in your groups and friend pairs see your name and what you add there.
  • Service providers that process data for us: cloud hosting and storage, Google and Apple for sign-in, and Apple Push Notification service. They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your name, email, UPI ID, your Google or Apple sign-in link (and revoke Sign in with Apple), devices, sessions and notifications. Shared expenses and the coin ledger are kept without your personal details, because other people's balances and records depend on them.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details in the app, delete your account in Account → Delete account, withdraw consent by deleting your account, and ask us about your data or raise a grievance at {email}. We'll reply within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Bill photos are stored privately and only shown to members of the group they belong to.

Changes

We'll tell you in the app about significant changes to this policy.
